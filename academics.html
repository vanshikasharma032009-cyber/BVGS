Our Curriculum

CBSE Senior Secondary school (Affiliation No. 531726), offering co-educational, English-medium learning through Class 12 (10+2).

Pre-Primary (Nursery - UKG)

Age Group: 3-6 years | Focus: Play-based learning, socialization, foundational skills

Our pre-primary program creates a joyful first school experience through activity-based learning, phonics, creative arts, music, and outdoor play. We focus on developing motor skills, language, cognitive abilities, and emotional intelligence in a nurturing environment.

Play-way & Montessori methods Phonics & early literacy Numeracy through activities Creative arts, music & dance Storytelling & puppetry Outdoor play & nature walks Celebration of festivals Parent-teacher partnership

Primary (Class 1-5)

Age Group: 6-11 years | Focus: Strong academic foundation, character building, exploration

The primary years build strong fundamentals in core subjects while encouraging curiosity and creativity. Our CBSE-aligned curriculum emphasizes conceptual understanding over rote learning, with regular assessments to track progress.

English, Hindi, Mathematics Environmental Studies (EVS) Computer Science basics General Knowledge & Value Education Art, Craft & Music Physical Education & Yoga Library & Reading Program Continuous assessment (CCE)

Middle School (Class 6-8)

Age Group: 11-14 years | Focus: Subject specialization, critical thinking, leadership

Middle school introduces subject-specific teachers and laboratories. Students develop analytical skills through project-based learning, participate in Olympiads, and explore leadership roles through student council and clubs.

Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) Social Science (History, Geography, Civics) Sanskrit / French (Third Language) Advanced Mathematics Science & Computer Labs Olympiad & NTSE preparation Life Skills & Value Education Student Council & Clubs

Secondary (Class 9-10)

Age Group: 14-16 years | Focus: CBSE Board preparation, stream guidance, career awareness

Rigorous board exam preparation with expert faculty, regular mock tests, and personalized attention. Career counseling helps students choose the right stream for Class 11. Remedial classes and doubt-clearing sessions ensure no child is left behind.

CBSE Board Exam Preparation Science, Math, Social Science, English, Hindi Information Technology (Optional) Practical Lab Work & Projects Monthly Tests & Pre-Boards Career Counseling & Stream Selection Remedial & Enrichment Classes Time Management & Study Skills

Senior Secondary (Class 11-12)

Age Group: 16-18 years | Focus: Specialization, competitive exams, university readiness

Senior Secondary education is available in Science, Commerce, and Humanities. Specialized coaching is offered for NEET, JEE, and NDA.

Science: PCM / PCB with JEE/NEET coaching Commerce: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics Humanities: History, Political Science, Psychology, Geography NEET, JEE & NDA coaching

Co-Curricular & Sports

Focus: Holistic development, talent discovery, team building, leadership

Education extends beyond classrooms. Our extensive co-curricular program includes sports, performing arts, visual arts, STEM clubs, and community service. Students participate in inter-school, district, state, and national level competitions.

Sports: Cricket, Football, Basketball, Athletics, Badminton Performing Arts: Music, Dance, Drama, Theater Visual Arts: Painting, Sculpture, Craft, Photography STEM: Robotics, Coding, Science Club, Math Club Literary: Debate, Elocution, Creative Writing, MUN Clubs: Eco Club, Heritage Club, Interact Club Community Service & Social Outreach Educational Trips & Excursions
